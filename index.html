// 02 · THREE STEPSFLEXIFLOW

From glass reactor to recipe-driven run.

Designed for automating existing lab-scale jacketed glass reactors.

01

Connect

Wire RTDs, analog and digital I/O, and serial instruments into FlexiConnect. Front LEMO for sensors and actuators; rear panel for communications.

02

Run the recipe

Load a crystallisation or process recipe. FlexiConnect executes ramps, holds, seeding and dosing locally.

03

Keep the record

Events, sensor values and operator actions are written into an audit trail designed toward electronic-record requirements.

Design principles

Built around fail-safe behaviour.

01

Sensor loss never counts as done.

A missing reading stops the step. It never completes it.

Under development
02

A short dose blocks the next step.

No moving on until the intended amount is in.

Under development
03

No automatic resume after power loss.

A person decides what happens next.

Under development
04

Independent hardware safety stop.

In the design, even if software does not respond.

Under development
Long-term vision

Our long-term vision is physical AI for laboratory automation: machines that sense, interpret process context and act through controlled execution. Today FlexiFlow automates recipes and records every run. The rest is the direction we are building toward.
