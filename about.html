We started as an automation and IoT company, with around eight years of experience in industrial IoT, machine connectivity, embedded systems, industrial automation and solution architecture, integrating physical equipment with software platforms and building automation for industrial environments.

Then we hit a real requirement: automating lab reactors. We did what everyone does and built it with SCADA, HMI and PLCs. But the recipe engine, with the dynamics scientists expected, couldn't be achieved that way.

So we asked: why not build it properly? We researched what scientists actually need, and the answer was a product that buys back their time. That is how FlexiFlow began.
