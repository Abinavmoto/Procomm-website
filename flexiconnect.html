Front panel

LEMO connectors

Direct ties into the reaction: temperature, analog loops and discrete I/O.

  • RTD inputs 2×
  • Analog in (AI) 2×
  • Analog out (AO) 2×
  • Digital in (DI) 2×
  • Digital out (DO) 2×
  • Notification LED run state
Back panel

Communications & power

Serial and fieldbus on a DB15, plus dedicated network and service ports.

  • RS232 2× via DB15
  • RS485 1× via DB15
  • CAN via DB15
  • Ethernet 2× RJ45
  • USB service / local PC
  • Add-on modules extra RTDs & comms
