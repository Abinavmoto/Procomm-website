// OUR STORYPROCOMM DYNAMICS

Procomm Dynamics is developing hardware-led automation for laboratory reactors, combining instrument connectivity, local control and recipe-based software.

Our approach draws on experience in industrial IoT, embedded systems, machine connectivity and automation. FlexiConnect is our developing controller and instrument interface; FlexiFlow brings recipe planning, supervision and experiment records together.

We are building toward Machines with Context: scientific instruments that can respond to process information within defined control and safety boundaries. Hardware compatibility and feature availability remain subject to verification.

Based in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India.
