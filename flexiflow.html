FlexiFlow

Recipe automation.
Supervision. Records.

FlexiFlow is the product: recipe-driven control for the jacketed glass reactor you already own, with FlexiConnect as the physical controller and instrument interface.

01

Recipes

Create and version recipes. Ramps, holds, seeding and dosing live as structured steps the controller can execute.

02

Access

Role-based access and user management are part of the product design, so who can change a recipe is controlled.

03

History

Timestamped events, process histories, operator actions and experiment records sit on one timeline per run.

Records note. FlexiFlow is designed around audit trails and electronic-record requirements. 21 CFR Part 11 alignment is a development objective. It is not a validated or certified claim.
