Cooling + antisolvent crystallisation

Adapted from Pickles et al., CrystEngComm 2024 · 7 steps · ~9 h · illustrative simulation

crystallisationtemperaturedosing
  • 1Ramp to 75 °C at 1 °C/min
  • 2Hold 30 min
  • 3Cool to 60 °C at 0.2 °C/min
  • 4Seed and hold 30 min
  • 5Dose 33.33 mL antisolvent over 45 min
  • 6Hold 30 min
  • 7Cool to 5 °C at 0.2 °C/min

Example recipe adapted from published research. Illustrative, not a validated procedure.

// STEPSFLAGSHIP PROFILE
01–02

Heat and hold

Ramp to 75 °C at 1 °C/min, then hold 30 min.

03–04

Cool, seed, hold

Cool to 60 °C at 0.2 °C/min. Seed. Hold 30 min.

05–07

Dose and finish

Dose 33.33 mL antisolvent over 45 min, hold 30 min, cool to 5 °C at 0.2 °C/min.

Design principles

Fail-safe behaviour in the design.

01

Sensor loss never counts as done.

A missing reading stops the step. It never completes it.

Under development
02

A short dose blocks the next step.

No moving on until the intended amount is in.

Under development
03

No automatic resume after power loss.

A person decides what happens next.

Under development
04

Independent hardware safety stop.

In the design, even if software does not respond.
