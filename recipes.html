Cooling + antisolvent crystallisation

Pickles et al., CrystEngComm 2024 · 7 steps · ~10 h · illustrative simulation

Example recipe adapted from published research (Pickles et al., CrystEngComm 2024). Illustrative, not a validated procedure.

// STEPSFLAGSHIP PROFILE
01–02

Heat and hold

Ramp to 75 °C at 1 °C/min, then hold 30 min for dissolution and equilibration.

03–04

Cool, seed, hold

Cool to 60 °C at 0.2 °C/min. Seed. Hold 30 min.

05–07

Dose and finish

Dose 33.33 mL antisolvent over 45 min, hold 30 min, cool to 5 °C at 0.2 °C/min.

Fail-safe principles

Built into every recipe run.

01

Sensor loss never counts as done.

A missing reading stops the step. It never passes it.

02

A short dose blocks the next step.

No moving on until the right amount is in.

03

No automatic resume after power loss.

A person decides what happens next.

04

Independent hardware safety stop.

Works even if the software does not.
