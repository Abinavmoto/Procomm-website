How it works

The recipe runs on the controller.

Safety is independent of software. Every step and sensor value is logged and signed.

01

Recipe on-device

The crystallisation or process recipe executes inside FlexiConnect. The C99 engine steps ramps, holds and doses at a 10 ms tick, beside the reactor, not in a remote service.

02

Independent safety layer

A hardware safety stop sits outside the control software path. If software misbehaves, the stop still works.

03

Signed record

Every setpoint, reading, quality flag, dose and operator action is written into a Part 11-style audit log on one timeline.

// CLOSED LOOPSENSE · DECIDE · ACT

What happens each tick.

  • 01

    Sense

    Read RTDs, analog loops and digital lines. Attach a quality flag. Timestamp everything.

  • 02

    Decide

    Compare against the recipe step. Extend a hold, slow a dose, or enter a safe state when inputs are wrong.

  • 03

    Act

    Drive AO/DO and serial instruments. Write the reason into the audit log.

Fail-safe principles

Four rules that never bend.

01

Sensor loss never counts as done.

A missing reading stops the step. It never passes it.

02

A short dose blocks the next step.

No moving on until the right amount is in.

03

No automatic resume after power loss.

A person decides what happens next.

04

Independent hardware safety stop.

Works even if the software does not.
